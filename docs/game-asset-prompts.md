# Chriesisturm game: generation prompts for every asset

Prompts in the order they are needed. Everything sits on the printed spread as a **miniature
diorama** (AR), seen from above and behind the runner. Readable silhouettes and clear colours
matter more than detail. Sizes are real-world metres; the code scales them onto the page.
Placement, node names and clip names: `docs/game-assets.md`.

## Shared style (paste in front of every 3D prompt)

> Stylized low-poly miniature for a children's storybook diorama, Swiss papercut look, soft matte
> flat colours, clean chunky shapes, gently rounded edges, hand-painted texture, warm daylight,
> readable silhouette from above, no text, no logos, single object centred, plain background.

Palette: cherry red `#C8263A`, leaf green `#5E9E4A`, Zug blue `#2A6FB5`, cream `#F4EEDC`, wood `#A8743F`.

**Negative prompt (all 3D):** photorealistic, high detail noise, text, letters, logo, watermark,
multiple objects, base plate, ground, scenery, harsh shadows, dark, scary.

Export: GLB, Y up, origin at the bottom centre, Draco + KTX2. Budget: characters ≤ 5k tris,
props ≤ 1k, buildings ≤ 2.5k. Texture 512 px for props, 1024 px for characters and buildings.

## A · Characters

### 1. `raceRunner`: the player (rigged)
Size ≈ 1.6 m · faces −z · T-pose for auto-rigging

> A cheerful Swiss child, about nine years old, ready for a cherry ladder race: white t-shirt
> with one Zug-blue horizontal stripe, cherry-red shorts, white-and-blue headband, sturdy
> running shoes, rosy cheeks, short brown hair. Full body, T-pose, arms straight out to the
> sides, legs slightly apart, facing front.

Rig: hips, spine, chest, neck, head, upper/lower arms, upper/lower legs, feet. Clips (lowercase):
`run` (loop, right hand raised to hold a ladder on the shoulder), `jump`, `slide` (feet first,
leaning back), `stumble`, `idle`, `climb` (loop), `pick` (reaching up), `cheer` (both arms up, loop).
Add an empty named `carry` on the right shoulder.

### 2. `raceRival`: the four rivals (same rig and clips as the runner)
Make four variants and deliver whichever one we use, or all four (`raceRival_0` … `_3`).

> Same style and proportions as the player child, a different racer: (a) boy in a sun-yellow
> shirt and green cap · (b) girl with two braids in a leaf-green shirt · (c) tall boy in a purple
> shirt and white knee socks · (d) girl in a Zug-blue shirt with a red bandana. Full body,
> T-pose, facing front.

### 3. `raceCrowdPerson`: spectator (instanced hundreds of times)
Size ≈ 1.5 m · **≤ 300 tris, one mesh, one material, no texture** (the code tints each copy)

> Very simple stylized spectator figure, rounded body and head, both arms raised in a cheer,
